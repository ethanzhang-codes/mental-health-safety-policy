# Ethics and Safety

The repository supports only an offline research/decision-support framing. Policy actions are labels in CSV outputs; no code initiates contact with a human, emergency service, or external system. “Urgent escalation” is an implemented fourth output level, not a real intervention.

False negatives risk inappropriate continuation during crisis evidence. False positives risk distress, loss of trust, unnecessary intervention, and unequal burden. Automation bias is a concern because a numeric level and fluent rationale may be mistaken for clinical judgment. Rationales are templates explaining triggers, not diagnoses or validated causal explanations.

The repository does not document IRB status, consent/waiver determinations, data-use agreements, storage controls, de-identification procedures, demographic subgroup evaluation, or clinician/human-factors review. These require author confirmation. Existing datasets may involve sensitive disclosures even if public or de-identified; raw text should be minimized and access controlled.

The system is not clinically validated, prospectively evaluated, deployment-ready, or a medical device. It should not be described as diagnosing a condition, predicting real-world crisis, or replacing professional judgment. The supported claim is offline escalation-policy research using dataset-defined Alert/Confirm evidence and proxy policy targets.
